    How the numbers work

    Everything is read at the minute on the timeline. A building banks its first-control bonus the moment you take it, then its per-minute rate for every minute after that, so drag the timeline to any point to see the total you would be sitting on. The full map runs at 7,560 alliance points per minute, but only 5,280 of that exists before 15:00. Mark a building Enemy holds and it banks for them on the same maths, so the two totals read against each other. All of it assumes nothing changes hands after the minute you set, so treat both sides as ceilings — and kills, Baggage Trains and Undercellars are income on top.
